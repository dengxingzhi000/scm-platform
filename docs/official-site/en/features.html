Capability,
by domain.

Not a service inventory — an answer to “what problem does this solve”. Each domain lists only endpoints and constraints that exist in the code today. Capabilities that are not wired do not appear here.

01

Supplier & Procurement

ProblemWhen admission, certificates, contacts, accounts and evaluations live apart, the supplier has no single truth.

SRM / Purchase
Admission

Admission flow

Draft → submit → review (approve/reject). Only approved, non-blacklisted suppliers can be enabled; the tax id must be complete; every change is written to the change log.

Certificate

Certificates

Validity is classified as valid, expiring, expired or rejected from the expiry date. Review, expiry query and counts feed the supplier overview.

Risk

Risk & cooperation

Risk levels 1–3; cooperation states 0–3 move along legal paths. Blacklisting disables the supplier and terminates cooperation; removing it does not rewrite admission history.

Performance

Performance scorecard

Evaluations weight quality, delivery, service and price; the total is written back to the supplier. The scorecard aggregates by dimension and suggests a grade.

Profile

Profile & master data

The profile aggregates master data, contacts, bank accounts, certificates, categories and recent changes. The first contact or account becomes default; switching clears the old default.

Procurement

Procurement & settlement

Purchase plans, RFQs, quotations, price comparison, contracts, purchase orders and receipts; supplier statements support create, confirm and mark paid.

02

Inventory & Warehousing

ProblemAvailable-to-promise must answer at the moment of order; the ledger must still reconcile.

Inventory / WMS
Atomic

Redis Lua deduction

Deduction, restock and flash-sale counters run atomically in Redis. The hot path does not query PostgreSQL; stock cache keeps a short TTL.

Reservation

Reserve and release

Reservations default to 900 seconds; a successful payment confirms and converts them to a real deduction; cancel or timeout releases; an expired-release endpoint feeds the scheduler.

Adjustment

Adjustment & transfer

Inbound, outbound, stock-take adjustments and cross-warehouse transfers are idempotent by business number; retries do not double-post.

Alert

Stock alerts

Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts support paging, statistics, resolution and a full scan; alerts clear automatically when stock recovers.

Snapshot

Daily snapshots

Snapshots are generated by date, SKU and warehouse and support trend queries. A repeat run for the same day skips existing rows.

Warehouse

Warehouse operations

Inbound/outbound orders, receiving, shipping, cancellation, locations and wave picking; only orders that have not been received or shipped may be edited.

03

Orders & Fulfillment

ProblemWith enough statuses, an unauthorized or illegal transition becomes a data incident.

Order / Fulfillment
State machine

Only legal paths

PENDING_PAYMENT → PAID → PENDING_SHIP → SHIPPED → IN_TRANSIT → DELIVERED → COMPLETED. Cancellation is only from pending-payment or paid; terminal states stay terminal.

Aggregate

Validation in the aggregate

OrdOrder.transitionTo() calls canTransitionTo() and validNextStatuses(). Illegal transitions are rejected in the domain layer, not patched by a controller.

Dashboard

Order dashboard

Today, last 7 days or last 30 days, compared with the previous period or year-over-year. Revenue and order count are bucketed by day (or hour), with status counts and backlog; trend points carry time ranges for drill-down.

Contract

Pagination & validation

Order, order center and fulfillment share a paging shape; write endpoints use @Valid; the raw page echo is normalized by the frontend contract layer.

Order center

Commerce order center

Create, pay, ship, deliver, receive and cancel; shares tenant, permissions and audit with the supply chain orders.

Fulfillment

Fulfillment path

Pick → pack → ship (waybill and carrier recorded) → sign off. Cancellation records a reason; tracking events and the latest event are queried separately.

04

Logistics & Delivery

ProblemWhen waybills, tracking, routes and coverage live apart, the fulfillment result cannot be explained.

TMS
Waybill

Waybills

Create, query by order or waybill number, update status, cancel and logically delete; cancellation records the reason and operator.

Tracking

Tracking

Tracking nodes are reported, queried by waybill or number and paged. Fulfillment can also read the latest tracking event on its own.

Route

Routes

Rider, delivery date and route status with CRUD; routes page by delivery date.

Coverage

Delivery coverage

Carrier delivery areas are maintained and used to check whether a province/city/district address is covered.

Carrier

Carriers

Waybills are linked to carriers; waybill status and tracking are kept separate from order status.

Boundary

Responsibility boundary

Logistics owns the transport facts. Whether an order advances remains a decision of the order aggregate.

05

Finance & Money

ProblemBetween documents, payments, reconciliation and service fees, the difference must land on one row.

Finance / Payment
Settlement

Settlement & invoice

Settlement orders and items, invoice paging (optionally by counterparty) and freight rules; financial data stays inside the same tenant scope.

Reconciliation

Reconciliation workbench

Creating a record computes the amount difference; records are filtered by period, status and difference flag; reconcile, confirm and record a difference reason.

Service fee

Platform service fee

Bills support create, update, delete, a pending list and payment registration by bill; the tenant side provides rate configuration and a calculation job.

Payment

Payment orders

Create, query, page and cancel. A duplicate request for the same order and channel returns the existing pending payment instead of creating another.

Callback

Callback verification

Alipay RSA2 with app_id/seller_id checks; WeChat v3 signature header, a 5-minute clock window and AES-GCM resource decryption. Amount and channel must match the local payment.

Refund

Refund & idempotency

Refund orders and service; a payment only moves pending → paid; a repeated paid callback is treated as a no-op.

06

Platform Governance

ProblemIf permissions, approval, audit and notifications each invent their own tenancy, the boundary leaks once per service.

Tenant / Governance
Tenant

Tenant

Lifecycle, plans, quotas and platform rates; the tenant id enters the request context, SQL predicate and async threads.

Access

Permissions

Users, roles, permissions, departments and dictionaries; URL-level permission checks; if the permission lookup fails, the request is denied rather than allowed.

Approval

Approval

Flowable BPMN: start, approve, reject, pending tasks and process status; permission-change approval has its own entry point.

Audit

Audit

The @AuditLog aspect collects records. Writes require an internal HMAC-SHA256 signature; there is no HTTP delete endpoint; high-risk actions are mirrored to the sensitive-operation table.

Notify

Notifications

Templates, channel preferences and notification audit; in-app messages page, expose unread counts, mark one read and mark all read.

Message

Event publishing

An event-publishing service (scm-message): domain events are persisted through an outbox and delivered to Kafka for consumers such as analytics. In-app messages live in scm-notify, kept separate from notification audit.

07

Documents & Files

ProblemOnce contracts, documents and attachments have versions, “current” and “who changed it” must have answers.

Document / File
Template

Template versions

Version list, create a new version with an incrementing number, publish while archiving the previously published version and updating the current version.

Variable

Variable schema

Template variable schemas can be saved and queried; the definition must be a valid JSON array, so rendering can validate before it runs.

Render

Rendering

Render a document from a template and variable data; a manually registered document starts as generated.

Version

Document versions

Documents page, expose detail and allow manual registration; versions are listed newest first, preserving every render result.

Trail

Audit trail

Generate, render, sign and archive actions are written to the document audit trail; signing and archiving also change the document status.

File

Files

Metadata paging, a 30-minute presigned URL, create and delete. scm-file uses the MinIO client and is compatible with S3/OSS.

08

Data & Analytics

ProblemA transactional database is the wrong place for period aggregation, and reports must not press on the order database.

OLAP
Layers

ODS → DWD → DWS → ADS

Four models in ClickHouse: ODS keeps the raw CDC payload, DWD holds detail, DWS aggregates and ADS serves dashboards. Ordered by tenant_id, partitioned by month.

Ingest

Kafka ingest

A Kafka consumer writes ODS with the Kafka offset and timestamp. ReplacingMergeTree and SummingMergeTree are chosen per table.

Query

Declarative query

Metric, dimension, dataset and field metadata; SQL is generated from dataset and metric definitions, executed and filtered by organization scope.

Governance

Governance

Data lineage registration and upstream/downstream queries, data quality evaluation and materialization management.

Dashboard

Dashboards

The order dashboard aggregates inside PostgreSQL instead of shipping pages of transactions to the browser; period analysis runs on ClickHouse.

Retention

Retention

ODS 1 year, DWD 2 years, DWS/ADS 3 years. Business side: audit 2 years, login 1 year, API logs 90 days, notifications 6 months.

09

Commerce layer

ProblemConsumer-facing members, promotions, mall and payments must share tenancy and permissions with the supply chain base.

Commerce
Member

Members

Registration, profile, level, point accrual and ledger, delivery addresses and default address; duplicate registration returns the existing member.

Coupon

Coupons

Template issuance uses an atomic database decrement; usability is checked against the order amount; redemption is idempotent and repeated redemption returns the used result.

Campaign

Group buy & flash sale

Start and join a group, list members; list active flash sales, check stock and place a flash-sale order.

Mall

Mall

Seller onboarding, cart add/update/remove/clear and product reviews with paged queries.

Search

Search

Product search, similar products and personalized recommendations; single and batch indexing, full rebuild and removal by id.

Order center

Order center

Create, pay, ship, deliver, receive and cancel; paged queries filter by order number, user and status.

10

Console

ProblemBackend capability without a coherent operating path is capability nobody can use.

Next.js
Routes

50+ routes

Dashboard, product, order, inventory, warehouse, logistics, supplier, purchase, finance, notifications, tenant, approval, audit, document, file and analytics.

Stack

Target stack

Next.js 16.4 App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4 and shadcn/ui; TanStack Query v5 and TanStack Table v9.

Form

Forms

React Hook Form + Zod; long forms use zodResolver and a shared FormField.

Chart

Charts

Recharts for KPIs, ECharts for complex figures, G6 for topology; all lazy-loaded. AG Grid enters only through the DataGrid wrapper.

Contract

Contract layer

Backend DTOs live in contracts/ and feature services map them; the raw paged echo is normalized by contracts/page.ts.

Migration

Migration state

Ant Design 6 remains only on legacy pages. No new AntD pages; each domain moves to shadcn/ui incrementally.
